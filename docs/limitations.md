# Limitations

This AI-assisted research is offered for independent review. Computational-agent review does not establish completed external academic peer review. Human collaborators are identified by their supplied GitHub handles, Drefetr and magna-nz; see [contributor roles](../CONTRIBUTORS.md).

Finite-mode stability, finite-horizon identities, and restricted identification retain their stated assumptions. No general nonlinear stationary exact-memory theorem, Wilks law, universal detection threshold, global optimizer certificate, or physical inhibition discovery follows. Heavy tails, nonregular boundaries, nuisance estimation, reused reference banks, and adaptive development remain relevant.

The completed [S01 F continuation](F_followup.md) resolves the earlier missing procedure cells, profile meshes, and paired interaction summaries. It retains oracle calibration under a specified synthetic law. Missing-only numerical recovery is not uniform refitting, a fitted-null nested bootstrap, general size control, or certified resampling confidence coverage. The older F addendum's unresolved counts are historical rather than current limitations.

Regional comparisons are retrospective fitted-procedure comparisons. Previously examined catalogues, repeated use of held-out targets, overlapping training histories, retrospective episode rosters, and inspected catalogue revisions limit independent prospective interpretation. Preferred reported magnitudes mix source conventions; model eligibility is not proof of physical detection completeness. Geometry and fitted bandwidths do not identify physical interaction distances or location accuracy. Score gains against one reference need not persist against a stronger competitor. Conditional calibration is distinct from relative predictive preference.

LI and LI+ synthetic controls use declared generating laws and fixed nuisance backbones. Per-regime history denominators, negative outcomes, and numerical validation coverage must be retained. Results do not establish general recovery, nominal size, causal inhibition, or an operational earthquake forecast.

The regional raw records, exact selected cohorts, explicit JMA projection/adjudication, matched-generator histories and fitted states, and the primary known-law/S01 history bank are now supplied. The regional cohort reconstructions and complete frozen NZ refit have been executed, closing those input/replay gaps. Remaining reproduction work is study-specific: some original F restricted-fit states and full regional refitting routes remain unresolved. The earlier third-party manuscript's frozen catalogue, full logs and editable source are still unavailable; the reconstructed GeoNet snapshot is a separately identified input. The [replay guide](../reproduction/README.md) and dated execution records identify actual completed calculations.

Tests, claim reconciliation, document compilation, and visual checks have distinct scopes. They do not certify every theorem or numerical calculation. Source verification checks enumerated bindings rather than exhaustively identifying all manuscript claims. Historical TeX resources were not snapshotted, so exact historical layout/byte replay is not guaranteed. Current bounded check coverage is recorded in [validation documentation](../validation/README.md); a historical receipt is not evidence of a fresh run.
