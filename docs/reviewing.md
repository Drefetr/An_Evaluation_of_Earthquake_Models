# Independent review

Start with the [reviewer guide](../REVIEWER_START_HERE.md), the current [unified monograph](../paper/manuscript_unified.pdf), and the [limitations](limitations.md). The monograph distinguishes completed Stage 2 results from historical checkpoints. In particular, the [F scope note](F_followup.md) explains why October's unresolved outcomes no longer describe completed S01 coverage.

Inspect assumptions, observation laws, denominators, reference procedures, frozen training choices, and the declared validation roster before interpreting a score contrast. Preserve negative and inconclusive results. Conditional calibration, predictive comparison, physical identification, and prospective operational validation are different claims.

Use the manuscript ledger to trace displayed values to bundled authorities and the separate source inventory to inspect additional numerical provenance. Read acceptance receipts for their specific coverage. Source hashes, aggregate reconciliation, sampled numerical checks, and full raw replay provide different evidence. AI review or agreement among agents is not independent external academic peer review.

Report mathematical findings with assumptions and a derivation or counterexample. Report numerical findings with a minimal input, command, environment, expected result, and the relevant source identity. Distinguish document building, plot regeneration, fixed-history evaluation, and a full study. Do not attach third-party event records, private execution archives, or credentials to public reports.
