# F continuation: withdrawn from the current paper

The F continuation and its S01 recovery summaries have been removed from the current manuscript, abstract and conclusions. The recovery execution filled 1,528 missing cells and preserved 7,068 original cells, but the original restricted-fit states underlying those preserved cells could not be recovered. Saved scalar summaries alone do not meet this edition's evidence requirement for those fitted-procedure results.

The earlier fitted nuisance comparisons are a different study. Their original fit records were recovered, so their results remain in Chapter 7. They are not substitutes for the missing F states.

The [withdrawal record](../validation/withdrawals/CLAIM_WITHDRAWALS_20261105.json) lists retired claim IDs and reasons. Its [baseline archive](../paper/history/pre_withdrawal_e270a595.zip) preserves exact earlier manuscript sources and ledger. Withdrawn records still undergo source and arithmetic checks, but no longer count as current manuscript displays. The original [S01 internal review](../validation/acceptances/S01_ACCEPTANCE.json), protected authorities and [October F supplement](../paper/supplements/f_nuisance_addendum.pdf) retain their historical identities.

No fitted result was substituted, refitted or silently reclassified as a negative outcome. The current paper reports the supported earlier nuisance study, complete known-law replay, and separate local-inhibition controls.
