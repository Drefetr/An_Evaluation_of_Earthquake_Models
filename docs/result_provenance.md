# Result provenance and execution scope

The manuscript distinguishes original study execution, numerical audit, later public-input recomputation and document production. A recovered original result can be inspected without being newly recomputed. Supplying a runner also does not imply a fresh execution. This edition adds provenance and interpretation notes; it launches no new research fit, simulation or score-replay campaign.

Regional score contrasts are descriptive comparisons on inspected observations; their tables supply no confidence intervals for those contrasts. Numerical precision is not inferential precision. Synthetic Wilson intervals and MC standard errors retain the specific meanings stated beside their tables and are not removed by this distinction.

The F continuation and its S01 recovery conclusions were [withdrawn](F_followup.md). Their historical sources do not count as current results. Documentary source keys S01-S27 are a different naming scheme and remain in the source-availability map.
