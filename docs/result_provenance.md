# Result provenance and execution scope

The manuscript distinguishes original study execution, numerical audit, later public-input recomputation and document production. A recovered original result can be inspected without being newly recomputed. Supplying a runner also does not imply a fresh execution. This methodological edition adds one new bounded experiment: a train-only background-intercept fit on each of the 80 existing S04B histories. It also adds saved-record and analytic diagnostics. No new histories, inhibited fits or historical California fits were generated by this revision.

Regional score contrasts are descriptive comparisons on inspected observations; their tables supply no confidence intervals for those contrasts. Numerical precision is not inferential precision. Synthetic Wilson intervals and MC standard errors retain the specific meanings stated beside their tables and are not removed by this distinction.
