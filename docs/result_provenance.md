# Result provenance and execution scope


The [current publication coverage check](../validation/reproductions/score_replay_20261107/publication_refresh_20261108/independent_source_review.json) preserves the accepted historical replay results while binding their locations to the revised manuscript source. It is a source-custody refresh, not another likelihood replay.
The manuscript distinguishes original study execution, numerical audit, later public-input recomputation and document production. A recovered original result can be inspected without being newly recomputed. Supplying a runner also does not imply a fresh execution. The earlier methodological revision added one new bounded experiment: a train-only background-intercept fit on each of the 80 existing S04B histories. That revision also added saved-record and analytic diagnostics. No new histories, inhibited fits or historical California fits were generated by that revision.

The later selected-state replay campaign recomputed the declared historical likelihoods and reconciled the frozen table roster. Original fitted parameters, training selections and gates were retained; no historical optimizer campaign was repeated. The [independent coverage review](../validation/reproductions/score_replay_20261107/final_coverage/independent_review.json) separates newly reconciled fields, earlier replay evidence and scopes requiring no additional likelihood execution, including the retained exact-display rounding differences. The [CPU execution review](../validation/reproductions/cpu_20261107/checkout02_independent_review.json) separately records the bounded Linux controls and public-reader executions.

The separately versioned [refined CPU fitting entry point](../experiments/s04b_refined_cpu_reader/README.md) performs a fresh fit on one supplied history using the reviewed exposure-refinement adapter and the original fitting and checking rules. Its [source review](../validation/reproductions/continuation_20261107/joint_refined_cpu_combined_source_review.json) is separate from actual output acceptance. The default automatically extracts the audited Linux x86-64 shared library and original build receipt; it does not perform a fresh compilation. Ordinary use has a new finite local resource allowance and does not inherit the authors' expiry. An optional author execution record instead imposes the genuine dated campaign envelope. The case, result and disposition record identify the actual public entry point separately from the frozen numerical core. This route is not a historical selected-state replay, and the earlier bounded CPU acceptance did not execute this later reader.

Regional score contrasts are descriptive comparisons on inspected observations; their tables supply no confidence intervals for those contrasts. Numerical precision is not inferential precision. Synthetic Wilson intervals and MC standard errors retain the specific meanings stated beside their tables and are not removed by this distinction.
