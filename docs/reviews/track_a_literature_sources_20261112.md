## Metadata decisions

- WernerEtAl2011 is the California BSSA paper, not a forced attribution to Annals of Geophysics or to a different Werner paper.
- RhoadesEtAl2011 is Acta Geophysica 59(4),728--747; the DOI contains s11600, not the truncated s1160 found in an early report.
- Mignan2012 here is the sole-author JGR paper. The distinct Mignan--Woessner CORSSA tutorial is not conflated with it.
- Parsons2002 is explicitly identified by title and DOI because surname/year alone does not uniquely prescribe a work.
- Gardner--Knopoff metadata is checked against the actual scanned paper; the publisher PDF is listed alongside its university-hosted primary-paper copy.

The already present `Dieterich1994` is retained. The already present `GneitingRaftery2007` is retained and checked against the [authors' paper](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/people/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jasa.pdf).

## Explicit requested-journal corrections

- `HelmstetterKaganJackson2006` is **BSSA**, not SRL: [SCEC primary research record](https://central.scec.org/node/3700), DOI 10.1785/0120050067. The distinct 2007 SRL grid-forecast paper is not substituted for it.
- `Mignan2012` is explicitly the **JGR** completeness-distribution paper, not EPSL: [publisher record](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1029/2012JB009347). A brief search did not identify a matching Mignan2012 EPSL catalogue-completeness paper. The separate [Mignan--Woessner CORSSA tutorial](https://corssa.ethz.ch/export/sites/corssa/.galleries/articles-pdf/Mignan-Woessner-2012-CORSSA-Magnitude-of-completeness.pdf_2063069299.pdf) is also identified, not conflated.
- `WernerEtAl2011` is the **BSSA** California forecast paper, DOI 10.1785/0120090340. The exact title and authors are verified at the publisher; a different Werner CSEP-Italy paper is not used under this key.
- `RhoadesEtAl2011` is **Acta Geophysica**, DOI 10.2478/s11600-011-0013-5. Its paired T-test assumption is reported explicitly; the monograph's dependent-block sign-flip calculations are not presented as that test.

### Chapter 8: completeness and magnitudes

```tex
A magnitude threshold specifies the selected observations; it does not establish
complete detection or a homogeneous magnitude scale. Completeness estimates
have uncertainty \citep{WoessnerWiemer2005}, and a detection function can alter
the recorded frequency--magnitude distribution \citep{Mignan2012}. Changes in
that observation law can resemble changes in occurrence rate or productivity.
The fitted attenuation coefficient therefore describes the stated recorded-data
model; its common measurement and uncertainty interpretation is fixed in
Section~\ref{sec:diagnostic_scope}.
```

### Chapter 11: established forecasts and retrospective episodes

```tex
The competitive reference follows a long tradition of testable clustering
forecasts \citep{ConsoleMurru2001,HelmstetterKaganJackson2006,WernerEtAl2011}.
CSEP separates consistency of a specified forecast with target observations
from comparison of forecast scores \citep{Schorlemmer2007,RhoadesEtAl2011}.
Here the historical episode roster and evaluation archive were inspected
retrospectively. Causal training prefixes preserve temporal ordering within
each fit; they do not make the experiment prospective. The 2010 reversal and
the 2019 concentration describe variation across the declared target sets,
whose distinct episode and calendar denominators are retained.
```

### Chapter 14: stress shadows and proper scoring

```tex
Physical stress-shadow evidence is heterogeneous. Rate-and-state fault-state
calculations can explain suppression and apparent exceptions
\citep{HarrisSimpson1998}, while observations also motivate dynamic-triggering
interpretations \citep{FelzerBrodsky2006} and expose difficulties resolving
predicted decreases \citep{MallmanZoback2007}. The tested nonnegative radial
attenuation fields do not calculate those signed stress histories.
Their log-score contrasts evaluate the specified predictive laws on a common
recorded-data measure. Propriety concerns expected predictive reward
\citep{GneitingRaftery2007}; it neither converts a realized ranking into physical
attribution nor guarantees conditional adequacy. Residual and simulation
checks address that separate question \citep{BraySchoenberg2013}.
```
