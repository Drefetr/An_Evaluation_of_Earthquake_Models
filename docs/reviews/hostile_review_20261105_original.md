# Hostile review — unified monograph (`unified_monograph_final_release_20261101T104429Z` / repo HEAD `1226fc3`)

**Artifact reviewed:** `paper/manuscript_unified.pdf` (231 pages, SHA-256 `49b14630dd002a502edd98390af8bee31d5e115d67c2d14b04fab6dd42fd3f7f`)
**Date:** 5 November 2026
**Reviewer stance:** adversarial. The manuscript is unusually well hedged and its custody chain is strong. That makes the remaining integration defects more conspicuous, not less.

---

## Verdict

**Not release-ready as the edition it claims to be.** The body is scientifically careful and mostly internally consistent, but the **abstract omits the edition's headline result (S04B matched-generator controls) and reports only the mismatched S04 total-intensity negative**, which the paper's own matched experiment contradicts. There is also a direct **internal contradiction between §11.1 and Table 11.10** over whether EP beats OC in *every* California window, and the results chapter does not discuss the one episode where the strong benchmark loses. These are fixable editorial/integration defects, not a failure of the underlying analysis.

## Critical findings

### C1. The abstract misrepresents the S04B matched-generator result and omits it entirely

**Locations:** abstract (p. 3); §13.11 (pp. 159–160); §13.11.1 and Table 13.11 (pp. 161–164); §14.5 (pp. 171–172).

**Evidence.**
- The abstract says: *"The subsequent expanded fixed-backbone matrix evaluates magnitude-scaled immigrant-only and total-intensity inhibition. Magnitude-scaled immigrant-only inhibition gains in the broad/long synthetic regime while total-intensity attenuation has a negative mean gain there. ... Neither study establishes general detection or physical causality."*
- §13.11 states that this matrix is generated by the **original unit-mark, immigrant-only LI law** and that *"their contrast therefore measures performance under that specified generator; it does not establish relative power when magnitude-scaled or total-intensity inhibition generates the data."*
- The **matched** S04B experiment in §13.11.1 generates total-intensity histories. Table 13.11 reports LI+ total **mean +53.2332 nats, median +51.2906, 20/20 wins**, versus LI +42.5503 and LI+ immigrant +49.2902. The abstract's *"total-intensity attenuation has a negative mean gain"* is therefore true **only under the mismatched S04 generator** the paper itself disclaims.
- The abstract contains **zero occurrences of "S04B"** and **zero of "matched-generator"**, while the edition is branded *"S04B matched-generator scientific completion edition"* in `website/document_metadata.json` and the README.
- *"Neither study"* is stale: there are now three studies — the historical eight-seed LI controls, the S04 unit-mark matrix, and the S04B matched matrix.

**Why this is critical.** A reader of the abstract alone would conclude that total-intensity inhibition fails in the broad/long regime. The paper's own matched experiment shows the opposite (+53.23 nats, 20/20). The abstract reports the disclaimed comparison as the finding and suppresses the matched comparison that resolves it. This is not a nuance; it inverts the edition's headline.

**Required fix.**
1. Add to the abstract: *"In the separate matched-generator extension (S04B), magnitude-scaled immigrant-only and total-intensity procedures gain on every broad/long history; both matched raw procedures lose on average under narrow/short generation."*
2. Qualify the existing S04 sentence: *"under the original unit-mark, immigrant-only generator (a mismatched comparison), total-intensity attenuation has a negative mean; the matched S04B experiment reverses this in the broad/long regime."*
3. Replace *"Neither study"* with *"None of these studies."*
4. Broaden the final scope sentence from *"inspected historical data"* to *"inspected historical data and declared synthetic generating laws."*

### C2. §11.1 claims EP wins "in every California and Japan window"; Table 11.10 shows it loses in 2010

**Locations:** §11.1 (p. 126); Table 11.10 (p. 134); §14.4 (p. 170).

**Evidence.**
- §11.1: *"fitted inverse-power ETAS (EP) exceeds Omori continuous (OC) on joint score in every California and Japan window."*
- Table 11.10, 2010 row: **EP −1059.1042**, **OC −1050.2614**. EP is the **worst of the four arms** and loses by **8.84 nats**.
- §14.4 itself says: *"The expanded California backtest (Section 11.12) has a distinct retrospective episode roster and variable procedure contrasts: **EP does not win every episode**."*

**Why this matters.** If "window" means the three primary chronological windows, §11.1 is technically true but ambiguous; if it means every reported California evaluation, it is false in 2010. Either way, the chapter overview does not distinguish "primary window" from "episode," and the 2010 counterexample is not discussed in §11.12. A hostile referee will read the sentence as a claim of uniform EP superiority and then find the 2010 row.

**Required fix.**
1. Change §11.1 to *"in every primary California and Japan test fold"* (or *"in the three primary California folds and three JMA folds"*).
2. Add a sentence to §11.12 explicitly reporting the 2010 reversal and its scope.
3. Make the abstract say *"EP wins each of the three primary California folds and three JMA folds, but does not win every episode of the retrospective five-episode backtest (2010 is a loss)."*

### C3. §11.12 presents the five-episode backtest without discussing the 2010 EP loss

**Location:** §11.12 (pp. 134–135) and Table 11.10.

**Evidence.** Table 11.10 is the only place the 2010 EP result appears. The prose discusses the pooled results, the active/complement partition, and Japan, but never mentions that EP is worst in 2010. The abstract's *"variable procedure contrasts"* is too vague to alert the reader.

**Why this matters.** The paper's central competitive claim is that EP is the stronger benchmark. The one episode where it loses is buried in a table. A hostile reviewer will call this selective reporting even though the discussion chapter later concedes it.

**Required fix.** Add a paragraph after Table 11.10: report the 2010 values, the 8.84-nat EP deficit, the active/complement split (75/50), and the implication that the EP advantage is not uniform across episodes. The abstract should name the exception.

## Moderate findings

### M1. §3.3.1 says the E04 supplement is unavailable, but it is in the public repository

**Location:** §3.3.1 (p. 32); `paper/supplements/explanatory_supplement.pdf`.

**Evidence.** §3.3.1: *"The worked event-free example and expanded derivations are provided in the separate E04 explanatory supplement; this archival supplement is **not included in the present delivery package**."* The public repository contains `paper/supplements/explanatory_supplement.pdf` (2 pages), and its page 1 contains exactly the referenced event-free derivation. The README lists the explanatory supplement as an available historical document.

**Why this matters.** The paper tells readers a derivation is unavailable when it is available. That is a factual error in a reproducibility statement.

**Required fix.** Say *"available in the public repository at `paper/supplements/explanatory_supplement.pdf`; it is not part of the sealed release package"* — or add the supplement to the release package.

### M2. The abstract's final scope sentence excludes the synthetic results it just reports

**Location:** abstract (p. 3).

**Evidence.** *"The conclusions concern declared fitted procedures on inspected historical data, not the existence or absence of physical inhibition."* The same abstract reports the S04 synthetic matrix and (after fixing C1) the S04B matched synthetic results. Synthetic generating laws are not "historical data."

**Required fix.** *"The conclusions concern declared fitted procedures on inspected historical data and declared synthetic generating laws..."*

### M3. The "six-target calendar-2019 complement" conflicts with Table 11.11's 16-target complement

**Location:** §11.7.3 (p. 137); Table 11.11 (p. 143); source `validation/authorities/682a9c7ecf12bc8c_approved_aggregates_v2.json`.

**Evidence.** §11.7.3: *"The six-target calendar-2019 complement has −0.777558 timing nats and −1.898745 OC-MC joint nats."* Table 11.11's 2019 row has **Complement N = 16**. The source authority has a `range: "calendar_2019"` contrast with `n_target: 6`, so the six-target row is a different partition from the 16-target fold complement. The text does not explain the relationship.

**Why this matters.** A reader will think either the text or Table 11.11 is wrong. The numbers are sourced, but the partition is ambiguous.

**Required fix.** Add a clause: *"a six-target subset of the 2019 block (distinct from the 16-target fold complement in Table 11.11)"* — or report the 16-target complement consistently.

### m2. S04B tables report only "Active" counts, not selected η

**Location:** Tables 13.8–13.11 (pp. 163–164).

**Evidence.** The tables report mean gain, MC SE, median, wins, Wilson interval, and an "Active" count. They do not report the selected η distribution. The paper's claims are score claims, not parameter-recovery claims, but a reader cannot see whether the matched procedure recovered the generating strength or merely improved score through nuisance adjustments.

**Fix.** Add a compact column or appendix table with median/range selected η per procedure and regime. State explicitly that this is not a recovery claim.

### m3. "Scientific completion edition" framing is stronger than the evidence

**Location:** title page/epigraph (p. 2), README, website metadata.

**Evidence.** The edition is labelled a "scientific-completion edition." The paper itself says the prospective programme is disabled, joint nuisance performance is not established, and external academic review is pending. The S04B matched test holds the nuisance backbone fixed.

**Fix.** Keep "completed bounded programme" and "scientific-completion edition" only if the abstract and front matter explicitly say the completion is of the declared programme, not of the scientific question. The current body does say this; the branding should not outrun it.

## What is actually solid

- **Mathematical scope discipline.** Theorems 3.1–3.6 and Propositions 4.2, 4.4, 5.1–5.3 are stated with explicit assumptions and scope limits; the paper does not claim a general nonlinear exact-memory theorem, Wilks asymptotics, or universal identification. The algebra I checked (Lyapunov drift, resolvent, tail exponent, pooling counterexample, score-bound constants) is correct.
- **Negative-result retention.** The paper retains the large uniform-null LI loss (−1.281445 nats/target), the narrow/short LI losses, the S04 total-intensity losses, the 2010 EP reversal in Table 11.10, and the failed Japan transfer criterion. This is unusually honest.
- **Custody and claim binding.** The claim ledger covers 27,033 records across 132 authorities; the S04B values (48.3868, 53.2332, −4.5674, etc.) are bound; `verify_claims.py`, `verify_repository.py`, and the 79-test suite pass. The numerical/custody chain is strong.
- **Statistical qualification.** The paper repeatedly distinguishes oracle calibration from fitted-null calibration, explanatory from confirmatory evidence, and retrospective from prospective evaluation. The adaptive-reuse disclosure is explicit.
