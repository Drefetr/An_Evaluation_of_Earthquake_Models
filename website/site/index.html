What the evidence means

The guide distinguishes mathematical validity, what can be learned from data, and performance on later observations. A good fit to past earthquakes does not by itself establish better forecasts.

The revised release

Original California completion, nuisance-fit states and source reports are now public alongside recovered catalogue inputs and completed replays. No California fits were repeated in this revision. Unsupported F results have been withdrawn from the paper and its conclusions.
