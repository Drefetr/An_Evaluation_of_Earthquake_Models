Understanding the earthquake research

How can we tell whether an earthquake encourages or discourages later activity - and whether modelling that distinction improves forecasts?

This collection explains the research in everyday language and provides the mathematical manuscript for readers who want the details.

What the evidence means

The guide distinguishes mathematical validity, what can be learned from data, and performance on later observations. A good fit to past earthquakes does not by itself establish better forecasts.

The revised release

The joint comparison is complete in all four regimes. Both arms fit the temporal backbone. The guide, document identities and findings explorer are refreshed for this edition; earlier incomplete publication records remain archived and their validators remain unchanged.
