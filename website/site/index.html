What the evidence means

The guide distinguishes mathematical validity, what can be learned from data, and performance on later observations. A good fit to past earthquakes does not by itself establish better forecasts.

The revised release

Of 80 planned joint-comparison histories, 78 completed; one optimizer-cap unknown and one wall-time failure remain unavailable. Against the jointly fitted uninhibited comparator, matched raw broad/long total inhibition averaged -1.2607 nats with 8/20 wins (95% Wilson win-fraction interval [0.2188, 0.6134]). Broad/long immigrant and narrow/short total full-regime summaries remain unavailable. Historical fixed-floor and intercept-only results are retained. Saved-state replay and bounded Linux CPU checks are documented separately.
