What the evidence means

The guide distinguishes mathematical validity, what can be learned from data, and performance on later observations. A good fit to past earthquakes does not by itself establish better forecasts.

The revised release

The original unit-mark narrow/short controls rejected no proposals in any of their 20 histories. Their losses are now interpreted as behavior on histories identical to same-seed uninhibited counterparts, not missed recovery of a realized inhibitory effect. The earlier S04B training-only background-intercept sensitivity and all historical score results remain available.
