Diagnostic Exponential Rate Attenuation in Spatial–Temporal Earthquake Models
Stability, identification and predictive evaluation of point-process models

Read the plain-language PDF or standalone HTML. The companion describes isotropic statistical rate-attenuation diagnostics, not a directional Coulomb or rate-and-state stress model. It distinguishes exact nested zero-strength ties from power tests, and descriptive multi-scale block summaries from calibrated significance tests.
