\subsubsection{Oracle ceiling and estimation loss}\label{sec:s8-oracle}
An oracle that knows the generating attenuation parameters but not the nuisance backbone achieves a mean held-out gain of 18.11~nats over the jointly fitted null (median 12.86, MCSE 4.18). Against a fixed true-backbone null the mean is 16.43~nats, and against an intercept-refitted null it is 14.54~nats. The fitted procedure captures only 0.294~nats on average; its mean estimation loss relative to the oracle is -17.82~nats (median -8.10, MCSE 6.08). The bottleneck is parameter estimation, not the absence of a true generating effect.
