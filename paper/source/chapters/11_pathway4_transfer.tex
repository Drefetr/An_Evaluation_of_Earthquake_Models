\section{Single-pass pre-sealed cross-regional transfer}\label{sec:pathway4-transfer}

Pathways~1--3 fixed the candidate recipes before any New Zealand or Japan test-window score was read. The frozen protocol records the interior-scale EP+ likelihood ($\sigma_0\geq0.05$~km, $\alpha_s\in[0,2]$, five-step training immigrant-probability KDE declustering), both observation-domain normalisations, the stationary $d=0$ and free-trend $d$ arms, the frozen monotone and annular Wells--Coppersmith attenuation profiles, all bounds and starts, and the test score decomposition. The operative protocol has SHA-256 prefix \texttt{32e0403d}; the training fits were written and hash-sealed in a manifest with SHA-256 prefix \texttt{06aa8df5} before the scoring scripts opened any New Zealand or Japan test array or archived score total. An initial freeze was invalidated before any test-window score was read because a sign/root-search error in the one-dimensional attenuation profile was found and corrected. The superseded protocol and twelve superseded New Zealand fits are retained in \path{experiments/pathway4_confirmatory_transfer/} and \path{validation/live_results/pathway4_confirmatory_20261116/superseded_fits_20261116/}. The independent audit verifies that the operative protocol predates every sealed fit and that no test score entered either freeze.

\paragraph{New Zealand.}
The New Zealand cohort contains $612$ targets in three folds. Table~\ref{tab:pathway4-nz} reports the EP+ minus archived EP and EP+ minus OC contrasts. The primary stationary full-plane arm gives a three-fold EP+ minus EP joint gain of $+111.346$ nats, with $+25.591$ from timing and $+85.755$ from location, and a positive joint contrast in all three folds. The EP+ minus OC joint total is $+161.618$ nats. The free-trend arm gives $+101.506$ nats over EP and $+151.778$ over OC. The rectangle-normalised stationary arm gives $+111.574$ nats over EP. These are procedure-level contrasts between separately fitted complete recipes; the two normalisations and trend laws are not additive components.

\paragraph{Japan.}
The Japan cohort contains $1{,}277$ JMA targets in three folds and nine annual blocks. Table~\ref{tab:pathway4-japan} reports the pooled contrasts. The primary stationary full-plane arm gains $+320.911$ nats over EP and $+542.123$ over OC. Its joint gain is location-dominated: $+330.456$ nats from location against a timing loss of $-9.545$ nats. The free-trend arm gains $+361.925$ nats over EP, with a positive $+62.528$-nat timing component and a $+299.397$-nat location component, and is positive in all three folds and in all nine annual JMA blocks from 2016 to 2024 (Table~\ref{tab:pathway4-japan-annual}). The rectangle-normalised stationary arm gains $+323.679$ nats over EP. The positive annual blocks are descriptive partitions of the same fitted state, not independent replications; the free-trend arm also pins the productivity coordinate at its upper bound and, in one fold, the trend at its lower bound.

\paragraph{Frozen attenuation profile.}
The confirmatory attenuation recipe is a one-dimensional profile on the frozen EP+ null backbone. The refined arm uses the Pathway~1 \texttt{ann2} kernel with the parameter-free Wells--Coppersmith scale $\ell_j=10^{-2.44+0.59M_j}$~km, $\psi=5$~d and $\gamma=0.75\log 10$; the exact nested state $\eta=0$ is retained. In Japan the training profile selects $\hat\eta=0$ in all three folds, so the attenuation refinement is observationally identical to the EP+ null backbone and its gain over that null is $+0.000$ nats. In New Zealand, Fold~1 selects exactly $\hat\eta=0$; Folds~0 and~2 select small positive training values ($0.0008$ and $0.0001$) and lose $-9.680$ and $-31.142$ nats on the test window, for a total of $-40.822$ nats relative to the EP+ null. Even with that attenuation loss, the refined state remains $+70.524$ nats ahead of archived EP. A zero attenuation gain is a selected boundary, not evidence for a physical stress shadow at the observed scale.

The single-pass result separates three claims. EP+ transfers as a complete fitted procedure to both external cohorts, with a location-dominated joint gain and, in Japan, a small timing loss under the stationary arm. The free-trend Japan arm reverses that timing sign and is positive in every annual block. The frozen attenuation refinement does not add held-out score: it selects zero strength in Japan and loses score in New Zealand. The California conditional calibration of the same interior-scale frozen states is reported in Section~\ref{sec:s05-completed}.
