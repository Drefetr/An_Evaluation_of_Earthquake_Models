\subsection{Trend--depletion controls in the first New Zealand fold}\label{sec:nz-trend-controls}
The first fold accounts for 16.518~nats of the 17.623~nat shared-attenuation deficit. A matched zero-trend fit reduces the first-fold loss to 8.504; freezing the uninhibited trend reduces it further to 4.556. Relative to the free fit, the zero-trend and fixed-null controls therefore remove 48.5\% and 72.4\% of the deficit, leaving a detrended residual of 8.504~nats. The positive fitted trend, not depletion alone, accounts for most of the original first-fold loss. The clamped-clock counterfactual is not a refit and produces a 24.791~nat loss; it is reported only to show that removing positive extrapolation without re-estimating the intercept is not an admissible control.

The middle fold's zero-trend fit selects the zero boundary and is tied at displayed precision, while the late fold retains a small positive strength. These two folds do not support the same attribution as the first fold. The autonomous zero-trend comparison in Table~\ref{tab:autonomous-nz} remains a separate fitted procedure with a different nuisance treatment.
