\subsubsection{A training-fitted uninhibited background}\label{sec:s04b-refitted}
The fixed comparator cannot lower its immigrant rate when broad/long inhibition reduces the event count. A separate sensitivity now fits its background intercept on each of the 80 existing training histories, holding the trend, excitation, spatial law, mark law and realized history fixed. The saved inhibited fits and original gates are unchanged. This is an executed comparator control, not a joint nuisance refit of both arms.

Writing $b=e^a$, precomputed event features $u_i,v_i$, background time integral $A$, fixed triggering exposure $H$ and common mark score $M$ give
\[ L(b)=\sum_i\log(bu_i+v_i)-bA-H+M. \]
This objective is concave in $b$. Endpoint derivatives or a bracketed derivative root select the global training optimum on the declared intercept box; that value is then frozen for test. The inherited box is $a\in[-12,0]$. All 480 phase--model checks against the historical floor scores pass. The protocol, executable scalar fit, per-history components and independent output review are supplied under \path{experiments/s04b_refitted_floor/} and \path{validation/live_results/methodological_20261106/}.
