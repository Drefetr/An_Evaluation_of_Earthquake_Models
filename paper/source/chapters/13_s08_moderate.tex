\subsubsection{A moderately thinned total-intensity control}\label{sec:s8-moderate}
The S8 generator uses $e^a=0.25\,\mathrm{day}^{-1}$, $\eta=0.4$, $\ell=15\,\mathrm{km}$ and $\psi=5$ days, with the preceding temporal-trigger and mark settings. The generating law is fixed before the twenty analysis histories are drawn. It rejects 27.21\% of event proposals and retains 94.7 test events on average (range $[78, 112]$). Both arms jointly fit $(a,d,n,c,p)$ on the 365-day training window; the total-attenuation arm also fits $(\eta,\ell,\psi)$. Its search includes the fitted null as an eligible state. The fitted parameters are frozen for the subsequent 365-day test window.
