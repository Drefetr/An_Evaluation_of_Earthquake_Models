Project-owned software, prose, figures, and derived result summaries have the separate licence scopes described in [LICENSES/README.md](LICENSES/README.md). ## New Zealand: GeoNet

[GeoNet catalogue documentation](https://www.geonet.org.nz/data/types/eq_catalogue), [WFS](https://wfs.geonet.org.nz/), [FDSN](https://service.geonet.org.nz/fdsnws/event/1/), and the [catalogue DOI](https://doi.org/10.21420/0S8P-TZ38) identify the external source. Read the [data policy](https://www.geonet.org.nz/policy) and dataset-specific terms when obtaining or redistributing inputs. GeoNet's site identifies CC BY 3.0 New Zealand for its content and requests provider/sponsor acknowledgement and appropriate dataset citation.

We acknowledge the New Zealand GeoNet programme and its sponsors for the data underlying the study. Project catalogue plots use derived aggregates; they are not a substitute for the external catalogue. The reconstructed historical snapshot and separate interface sensitivity are described in the [data guide](docs/data.md).

## Southern California: SCEDC and SCSN

The regional studies use preferred Southern California Seismic Network records distributed through the [Southern California Earthquake Data Center](https://scedc.caltech.edu/eq-catalogs/). Retain the provider's [citation policy](https://scedc.caltech.edu/about/citation.html), [SCEDC dataset DOI](https://doi.org/10.7909/C3WD3xH1), and applicable [SCSN network citation](https://doi.org/10.7914/SN/CI). Researcher-created alternate catalogues can have additional source-specific citation requirements.

The project-derived cohort selection, parser repair, fitted-procedure comparisons, and aggregates are described in the monograph. They do not imply provider endorsement or convert preferred reported magnitudes into a homogeneous magnitude scale. Historical snapshot identity and current provider availability are separate matters.

## Japan: Japan Meteorological Agency

The regional studies use annual hypocentre records from the [JMA Seismological Bulletin](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/eqev/data/bulletin/index_e.html), with [archives and format documentation](https://www.data.jma.go.jp/eqev/data/bulletin/hypo_e.html). Consult the [JMA legal notice](https://www.jma.go.jp/jma/en/copyright.html) and any specific rights notices. Retain source citation and identify edited/derived material as project processing rather than an official JMA product.

JMA's legal notice applies [Public Data License 1.0](https://www.digital.go.jp/en/resources/open_data/public_data_license_v1.0), subject to specific rights notices. The original annual hypocentre archives retain their source identity. The study's complete-case selection, source-JST boundaries, projection and derived scores are project processing, not official JMA products. The [data guide](docs/data.md) links the executable selection and recovered inputs.

## Publications and dependencies

The recovered earlier third-party manuscript PDF, cited papers, and full-text reference copies are excluded. Bibliographic citations and historical hashes identify sources without granting redistribution or relicensing rights.

No interpreter, dependency source tree, TeX cache, standalone fonts, or model weights are bundled. The CC BY 4.0 legal text comes from GitHub's published licence catalogue; its source is recorded in `LICENSES/source.json`. Model/product names identify the collaborators' reported workflow and do not imply endorsement.
