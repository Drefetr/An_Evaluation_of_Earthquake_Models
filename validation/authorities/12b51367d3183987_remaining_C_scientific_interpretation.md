# Remaining C: final scientific interpretation

Spatial routing improves the declared categorical earthquake-location forecasts, while the tested additional stress-depletion terms still do not improve aggregate held-out scores. W and D both completed their prospective schedules and independent raw-row audits: 60 observed procedures, 480 mandatory starts and 591 audited observed candidates. The fixed-seed engineering schedules also completed 40 procedures/320 starts. The same 612 held-out targets are reused across stages; these are not independent replications.

## Primary W result

A small global-redistribution component improves on strict local excitation by 10.553372 joint nats (+0.017244071/event), positive in all three windows. The gain is conditional-zone prediction (+13.789492), offset by worse regional timing (-3.236121). Selected omega values are approximately 0.0461,0.0452,0.0443. Omega is the mixture weight on global allocation, which can also return to the parent's zone, rather than a literal cross-zone offspring fraction.

Column-stochastic routing makes omega cancel from the ground intensity at fixed remaining parameters for excitation and both inhibition stress choices. Timing differences after joint refitting are indirect. Local/global-stress free-omega inhibition lose 3.637297 and 12.887727 nats against matched free excitation. There is no universal stress-localization rescue: at omega 0 the global-stress loss(-1.005026) is smaller than the local-stress loss(-3.399764); at omega 1 local stress selects eta 0 and numerically ties excitation. Eta 0 micro-differences should not be counted as meaningful forecast wins. See W_scientific_interpretation.md for the complete factorial reading.

## Primary D result and comparator limit

D uses nine fixed cells, actual parent coordinates to target-cell centers, normalized distance triggering and a separate unnormalized stress similarity. Training E alone chooses among 50,150,500,1500 km; all compared distance E/I models share that choice. Hierarchical half-count smoothing preserves zone mass and gives positive background support to unoccupied training cells.

Distance E beats the strict C 1-cell bridge by 74.843795 nats (+0.122293783/event), positive in all three windows. Its components are timing -3.259471, conditional zone -5.704315 and within-zone cell +83.807581 nats. Thus finer categorical allocation carries the benefit. Normalized E triggering has the same ground intensity at fixed parameters across scales; its timing changes after joint fitting do not imply a direct timing effect of routing.

The primary bridge is omega 0. It is weaker than W's already improved free-omega procedure, so the primary contrast alone cannot establish improvement over the best declared zone procedure. That limitation motivated the secondary common-space comparison below; it did not alter D's design, fits, scale choice or primary denominator.

Selected scales are 150,150,50 km. Fold 2 reaches the lower grid edge, and passing the cross-grid forecast-spread gate does not identify a physical scale. Local distance inhibition selects eta=3, the upper bound, in every fold. Its net loss is 2.275081 nats (-0.003717452/event), despite wins in two windows; the middle window's loss outweighs them. Global-stress inhibition loses 2.990767 nats. Results concern these bounded procedures, not an unrestricted eta optimum or an independently tuned stress range. Neither scale nor eta bounds were expanded.

## Secondary adaptive common-space comparison

This reporting comparison was planned after W and before opening D outcomes. It retains all seven D main models against one fixed reference: audited W E_wfree refined to cells by the already frozen training q(c|z). Refinement leaves W timing and conditional-zone scores unchanged, adds sum logq as the within-zone cell score, and allocates each cell compensator as q times its W zone compensator. It adds no fit, scale selection or test-based reselection.

Every comparison uses the same raw windows/order, beta and zone proportions. Independent review rechecked the raw-count q allocation, reference identities, all 21 per-window comparisons/seven aggregates, proper cell/zone E+C, and event-weighted rates. All original primary tables and denominators remain intact.

E_distance beats the refined W reference by 64.290424 nats (+0.105049712/event), positive in all three windows: +1.151609,+25.091636,+38.047178. Components are timing -0.023350, zone -19.493808 and within-zone cell +83.807581 nats. The timing difference is numerically small in practical score units, but this is not a formal equivalence/noninferiority claim. The result supports an additional within-zone categorical forecast benefit while showing no net timing gain.

The two distance inhibitory models also beat that coarse reference (+62.015343 and +61.299657 nats), but both remain worse than distance excitation itself. Beating a coarser comparator does not establish incremental inhibition. Global/bridge D baselines all lose against the refined W reference; every one of the seven models was retained rather than selecting a favorable subset.

This is explicitly secondary adaptive reporting, not an original primary endpoint or fresh confirmatory evidence. The events were already examined and reused. The raw point-process model supplies cell intensities, not a continuous position density or within-cell physical mechanism.

## Claim and scope decision

The claim register's exact primary and secondary numbers agree with the audited tables. Its limits concerning scale, all-three eta upper-bound estimates, categorical support, reused targets and causal/inferential exclusions are appropriate. The supported conclusion is improved coarse spatial allocation, with no demonstrated net timing improvement against the improved common-space zone reference and no aggregate benefit from the tested matched inhibitory additions.

No universal statement about stress localization, physical inhibition, true transfer distances, global optima, event-iid confidence intervals or Wilks boundary asymptotics follows. No new fit, bound/grid expansion, alternate inhibitory scale, kernel/productivity family, source change or further stage was introduced during final interpretation. Frozen historical authorities and primary artifacts remain intact.
